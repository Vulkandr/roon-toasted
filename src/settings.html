    Legend

    Toaster: The compact Roon player. Switch it off if you only use the Wrapper; its hotkeys and links then open the Wrapper instead. Open at Launch opens it whenever Roon: Toasted starts.

    Window Size: Default opens the Toaster at its normal size in the middle of the screen every time. Last Position reopens it where you left it, at the same size. Called "Remember Window" in the tray's quick settings.

    Zoom: When on, Ctrl + and Ctrl − (or Ctrl + mouse wheel) make the Toaster text bigger or smaller in 5% steps, and Ctrl + Enter resets to 100%. Now at 100%

    Auto-Hide: When on, clicking anywhere outside the Toaster sends it back to the tray, like pressing ×, along with Settings if open. When off, both get taskbar buttons. It starts working once a Roon Core is connected.

    Colors: Default keeps the Toaster's color scheme. Album Art takes its colors from the album that's playing.

    Hotkeys: Work anywhere in Windows, even when the Toaster is closed. Open Toaster opens it, or hides it if it's currently open. Search opens it on the Search tab, ready to type. If Hotkeys are disabled, Roon: Dialed Up can still bring up the Toaster.

    Start with Windows: Puts Roon: Toasted in the tray when you sign in, so hotkeys, toasts and the widget are always ready.

    The Wrapper: Shows the Roon app inside a Roon: Toasted window (Roon: Wrapped), with Toasted's player along the bottom, so everything Roon can do, its settings included, is right there. Open it from the tray menu, its hotkey or the button above; it starts Roon if needed. Closing it keeps Roon running out of sight, so it reopens instantly. Return Roon gives Roon its own window again, with an Enable Wrapper button in its title bar to wrap it back up. Open at Launch opens it whenever Roon: Toasted starts, at sign-in (with Start with Windows on) or by hand. Remember Player brings Now Playing back as you left it, even after a restart; off, the Wrapper always opens on Roon's page, as Roon does.

    Taskbar Widget: A small player on your main taskbar. Pick a side, then slide Position and Width until it sits in a free spot; One Line suits small taskbars. Windows doesn't officially allow this, so it may misbehave.

    Hearts & Library: Adds a button to the Playing tab: a "+" that puts the track in your library (the Add to Library switch decides whether that's just the song or its whole album), then a heart. The same buttons show on album pages by the playing artist. It uses a part of Roon that isn't officially open to apps, so a Roon update may break it until I'm able to repair it.

    Updates: Looks on GitHub for a newer Roon: Toasted (a minute after it starts, then every 6 hours) and shows it here and in the tray menu. Nothing downloads or installs until you press Install; the update installs the same way as the first install, then the app restarts.

    Toasts: A small card shows what's currently playing in the corner and on the monitor you pick, in the Toaster's colors. Primary Monitor follows whichever one Windows has as main. Clicking the toast opens the Toaster. Mousing over it pauses the timer.
