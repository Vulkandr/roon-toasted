    Legend

    Window Size: Default opens the Toaster at its normal size in the middle of the screen every time. Last Position reopens it where you left it, at the same size.

    Zoom: When on, Ctrl + and Ctrl − (or Ctrl + mouse wheel) make the Toaster text bigger or smaller in 5% steps, and Ctrl + Enter resets to 100%. Now at 100%

    Hide on Click Away: When on, clicking anywhere outside the Toaster sends it back to the tray, like pressing ×, along with Settings if open.

    Colors: Default keeps the Toaster's color scheme. Album Art takes its colors from the album that's playing.

    Hotkeys: Work anywhere in Windows, even when the Toaster is closed. Open Toaster opens it, or hides it if it's currently open. Search opens it on the Search tab, ready to type. If Hotkeys are disabled, Roon: Dialed Up can still bring up the Toaster.

    Toasts: A small card shows what's currently playing in the corner and on the monitor you pick, in the Toaster's colors. Primary Monitor follows whichever one Windows has as main. Clicking the toast opens the Toaster. Mousing over it pauses the timer.
