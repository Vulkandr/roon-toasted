Colors: Toasted keeps Toasted's own color scheme. Album Art takes its colors from the album that's playing. Custom uses an accent you pick. A Roon profile can have settings of its own, colors included (Wrapped's Settings, the wrench by the profile); while such a profile is in use, these are its colors.

Artists: Main shows only a track's first artist; All shows every artist Roon lists for it, everywhere in Toasted.

Auto-Hide: When on, clicking anywhere outside the Toaster sends it back to the tray, like pressing ×, along with Settings if open. When off, both get taskbar buttons. It starts working once a Roon Core is connected.

Start with Windows: Puts Roon: Toasted in the tray when you sign in, so hotkeys, toasts and the widget are always ready.

Skip Stuck Songs: Roon sometimes stops at the end of a song and never starts the next one. With this on, Toasted moves on to the next song after a few seconds stuck there.

          Hearts & Library: Adds a button to the Playing tab: a "+" that puts
          the track in your library (the Add to Library switch decides whether that's just the
          song or its whole album), then a heart. The same buttons show on album pages by the
          playing artist. A light next to the zone shows what's playing: Low Quality, CD Quality
          or Hi-Res (hover for the name). It uses a part of Roon that isn't officially open to apps, so a Roon
          update may break it until I'm able to repair it.
        
    
        
    
          Ban Button: Adds a button after Queue on the Wrapper's player that bans
          the song that's playing, for the Roon profile in use, as Roon's own ban does. It turns
          red while the song is banned; a second click lifts the ban.

    Living Queue
    
          
    
            Off
            
            On
          
    
          
          Requires Hearts & Library
        
    
        
    Press play on another zone to carry on where you were.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
          
    
          
    
            
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
        
    
          Legend
        
    
          Living Queue: One queue that follows you. Zones that take part all
          carry the queue of whichever one is playing. Press play on another of them and it
          carries on at the same song and the same second, and the zone you left is paused.
          Toasted has to be running for it to work, with Hearts & Library switched on
          (Roon Core).
        
    
        
    
          Off: The zone takes no part. Toasted never touches its queue.
        
    
        
    
          Mirrored: The zone always shows the song that's playing, ready to
          carry on. Its queue is replaced at every song change, which can keep the zone's
          device awake for as long as music plays on another zone that takes part.
        
    
        
    
          Hide Current: The zone is left alone, so its device can sleep. Once
          the music has moved on from the song it was left in, it shows a silent Living Queue
          track in its place. Press play on it and it jumps to the song and second you were at.

          Legend
        
    
          The Wrapper: Shows the Roon app inside a Roon: Toasted window
          (Roon: Wrapped), with Toasted's player along the bottom, so everything Roon can do,
          its settings included, is right there. Open it from the tray menu, its hotkey or the
          Open button above; it starts Roon if needed. Closing it keeps Roon running out of sight,
          so it reopens instantly. Return Roon gives Roon its own window again, with an
          Enable Wrapper button in its title bar to wrap it back up. Open at Launch opens it
          whenever Roon: Toasted starts, at sign-in (with Start with Windows on) or by hand.
          Remember Player brings Now Playing back as you left it, even after a restart; off,
          the Wrapper always opens on Roon's page, as Roon does.
