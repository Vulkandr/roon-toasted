    Legend

    Window Size: Default opens the Toaster at its normal size in the middle of the screen every time. Last Position reopens it where you left it, at the same size.

    Zoom: When on, Ctrl + and Ctrl − (or Ctrl + mouse wheel) make the Toaster bigger or smaller in 5% steps, and Ctrl + Enter goes back to 100%. When off, those keys do nothing. Now at 100%

    Hide on Click Away: When on, clicking anywhere outside the Toaster sends it back to the tray, like pressing ×, along with Settings if it's open. Moving between the two doesn't hide anything. When off, the Toaster stays open until you close it or press Escape.

    Colors: Default keeps the Toaster purple. Album Art takes its colors from the album that's playing and fades to the next album's colors when the track changes. Muted covers get a softer tint, and black-and-white covers a white accent.

    Hotkeys: Work anywhere in Windows, even when the Toaster is closed. Open Toaster opens it, or hides it if it's already in front. Search opens it on the Search tab, ready to type. Click a hotkey and press a new combination with Ctrl and/or Alt (Escape cancels). Clear turns one off; the Hotkeys switch turns both off, e.g. if they get in the way of other apps and you open the Toaster from the Stream Deck instead.
